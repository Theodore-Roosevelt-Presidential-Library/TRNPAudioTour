Theodore Roosevelt National Park, South Unit

The Scenic Loop
with Nora & Wes

A free, GPS-guided audio tour of the 36-mile drive. Two friends, a car full of stories, and the land that changed Theodore Roosevelt.

  1. Download the tour Cell service is spotty in the park. Do this on Wi-Fi before you go.
  2. Mount your phone On the dash or vent, where you can glance at it. Turn the volume up or connect to the car.
  3. Start at the South Unit Visitor Center In Medora. Pay the park entrance fee (card only), then tap Start.
